Istemaal ki sharaait

DEL PSX Downloader Open Ai

Aakhri tabdeeli: 8 November 2026

Zaati istemal

Yeh tool apne malik ki zaati company research aur official documents ko munazzam karne ke liye banaya ja raha hai. Yeh public investment service nahin hai.

Tool ka kaam

Tool official sources se company reports aur announcements hasil kar ke Google Drive ke manzoor shuda folders mein save karne aur download records rakhne ke liye hai. Setup aur testing abhi jari hai.

Data aur tasdeeq

Source website, internet, hosting limits ya authorization ke masail ki wajah se downloads late, na-mukammal ya nakaam ho sakte hain. Zaroori faislay se pehle asal official document aur us ki tareekh check karna user ki zimmedari hai.

Investment decisions

Tool buy ya sell orders execute nahin karta. Documents jama hone ka matlab kisi investment ki sifarish ya Shariah compliance ki tasdeeq nahin hai.

Ijazat aur control

User Google Account se app ki rasai revoke kar sakta hai aur cloud schedule band kar sakta hai. Pehle se save documents user ki Drive mein rehte hain jab tak user unhein na mitaye.

Original documents

Downloaded documents ke huqooq un ke asal publishers ke paas rehte hain. Yeh tool un documents ki ownership ya unhein dobara publish karne ki ijazat nahin deta.

Kharcha

Setup ki tarjeeh muft service allowances hain. Paid service ya paid usage activate karne se pehle user ki alag manzoori darkar hai. Service providers apni limits badal sakte hain.

Taalluq aur privacy

Yeh PSX, Google, GitHub ya OpenAI ki official application nahin hai.

Data ke istemal ki tafseel Privacy Policy mein di gayi hai.

Rabta
