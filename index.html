DEL PSX Downloader Open Ai

Open AI DEL Project ke liye zaati istemal ka company reports aur announcements jama karne wala tool.

Maqsad

Yeh tool Pakistan Stock Exchange aur official company sources se reports aur announcements hasil kar ke Google Drive ke manzoor shuda folders mein munazzam karne ke liye banaya ja raha hai.

Google Drive ka istemal

Google ki ijazat ke baad tool chunay huay folders aur files tak rasai istemal karega. Is ka maqsad reports save karna, pehle se mojood files pehchanna aur download ka record rakhna hai.

Maujooda haalat

Setup aur testing jari hai. Automatic cloud downloads abhi mukammal taur par live nahin hain.

Is website par kya hai?

Yeh sirf app ki maloomati website hai. Yahan portfolio, private reports, passwords ya Google connection credentials publish nahin kiye jate.

Yeh tool trade execute nahin karta. Yeh PSX, Google ya OpenAI ki official application nahin hai.
